# Creative direction

Build the theme from visible evidence instead of mapping every artist to one fixed stereotype.

## Derivation order

1. Sample the dominant background, clothing, light, and accent colors.
2. Identify composition: subject position, face focus, quiet text area, and likely `cover` crop.
3. Select one art direction: editorial monochrome, candy pop, stage neon, dreamy pastel, luxury minimal, retro Y2K, or cinematic dark.
4. Select two recurring motifs only, such as hearts + sparkles, chrome + stars, film marks + fine rules, or flowers + handwritten accents.
5. Write concise, original UI copy tied to the concept.

## Copy set

- `name`: artist/group × CODEX.
- `brandSubtitle`: era or art-direction label.
- `tagline`: one short creative sentence.
- `statusText`: compact mode indicator.
- `quote`: original microcopy, fandom slogan, album title, or a lyric excerpt of at most 10 words.
- `projectLabel`: one emoji plus artist/group projects.
- `projectPrefix`: one small motif.

Do not invent factual associations. If the artist, fandom, era, album, or lyric is uncertain, ask or use neutral original copy.

## Examples

- Black-and-white Jennie portrait: editorial monochrome, ruby accent, fine rules, `CREATE IT YOUR WAY`.
- Bright pink Jennie portrait: candy-luxury, crown + sparkle motifs, ruby/pink/cyan palette.
- Blue BTS group stage image: stage neon, chrome + stars, navy/cyan/violet palette, short ARMY-safe copy.

Examples are inspiration, not locked presets.
